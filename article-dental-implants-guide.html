Dental implants in Delhi: the questions to ask before you say yes

By Dr. Animesh Agarwal · Delhi Dental Cosmetics, GTB Nagar · 6 min read

A missing tooth is not only a cosmetic problem. The teeth beside it drift into the gap, the bone under it slowly shrinks, and chewing shifts to one side. An implant replaces the root of the lost tooth, which is why it behaves more like a natural tooth than any other option.

What an implant actually involves

A titanium post is placed in the jaw where the root used to be. The bone grows onto it over the following months and locks it in place. A crown is then fixed on top. Done properly, the finished tooth looks and functions like the one it replaced, and patients often forget within a few months which one it was.

Five questions worth asking any clinic

Which implant system? Established international brands have decades of published follow-up data. Ask for the name written down, because implant alone tells you nothing about what is going into your jaw.

Who does which part? Planning, surgery and the final crown may all be done under one roof, which keeps responsibility in one place. If parts are outsourced, ask who handles a problem later.

Is the bone sufficient? A proper X-ray before surgery is not optional. It decides whether the implant can go in directly or whether a bone graft is needed first. A plan made without an X-ray is a guess wearing a white coat.

What is the total cost, top to bottom? In Delhi a single implant with its crown typically lands somewhere between 25,000 and 55,000 rupees depending on the system and any grafting. Get the full figure, including the crown, in writing before starting. A low surgical quote followed by an expensive crown is a common pattern.

How long, honestly? A straightforward case runs about three to six months from placement to the final crown. Any clinic promising a fixed two-week timeline for every case is selling speed you may not want in your jaw.

Who is a good candidate

Most healthy adults are. Controlled diabetes is manageable with proper planning. Heavy smoking raises the failure risk and we say so plainly before starting. Age matters less than bone quality and general health. Even patients in their seventies get implants routinely when the bone allows it.

Caring for it afterwards

An implant needs the same care as a natural tooth: brushing, flossing, and a professional cleaning every six months. Implants do not get cavities, but the gum around them can get infected, and neglected gums are the main reason implants fail years later. The ones that last decades belong to patients who kept their cleaning appointments.

One of our patients travelled from Shamli for three implants, multiple root canals and twenty-eight zirconia crowns, and wrote afterwards that the trips were worth it. That is the standard worth holding any clinic to, including ours.

Before you visit

Questions patients actually ask

Timings, costs, children, emergencies. If your question is not here, the clinic answers the phone.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Appointments are required before visiting, so the doctor you need has proper time for you. Request a slot through the form on the Contact page, or call the clinic during working hours and we will fit you in.

Where exactly is the clinic?

2413 Basement, Hudson Lane, Kingsway Camp, GTB Nagar, Delhi 110009, about a minute from GTB Nagar Metro Station Gate No. 4. Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 1:30 PM and 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM. Sunday is closed.

What are the timings?

Monday to Saturday: 9:30 AM to 1:30 PM, then again 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM. Sunday is closed. Emergency pain is seen the same day whenever a chair is free.

Is the clinic wheelchair accessible?

Yes. The clinic is on the ground level of the building and wheelchair access is straightforward. Tell us in advance if you need help and someone will meet you at the door.

Which payments do you accept?

Cash, UPI and cards are accepted. Treatment costs are quoted in writing before work begins, so the figure you approve is the figure you pay.

How much does a consultation cost?

A first consultation with examination is nominally priced, and any treatment is quoted separately and in writing before it starts. Cost lines for each treatment are on the Treatments page.

What happens at a first visit?

A first visit is examination, X-ray if needed, and a clear explanation of what is going on and what the options are. Nothing is started the same day unless it is urgent and you agree to it.

Can I choose which dentist I see?

Yes. The booking form lets you request Dr. Animesh Agarwal or Dr. Neha Singhal, or leave it to whichever dentist has the right slot for your treatment.

Do you treat children?

Yes. First visits for children are kept short and friendly, and any treatment is explained to the parent before it begins. There is a full guide to a child's first visit on the blog.

What if I have dental pain right now?

Call the clinic. Same-day emergency slots are kept for pain, swelling and broken teeth. If it is after hours, the recorded message gives the next step.

Will I know the cost before treatment starts?

Always. Every plan is written down with the full figure before work begins, and nothing is added later without your approval.

Is my information safe?

The booking form collects only what is needed to confirm your slot: name, phone number, optional email, and your note. It is visible only to clinic staff. There is a plain-language privacy page linked in the footer.

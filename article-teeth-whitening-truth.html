Teeth whitening: what works, what does not, and what it costs in Delhi

By Dr. Animesh Agarwal · Delhi Dental Cosmetics, GTB Nagar · 4 min read

Walk past any pharmacy shelf and whitening promises compete for your money. Before spending on any of it, it helps to know what actually changes tooth colour and what only changes your opinion of the tube you bought.

Why teeth are not paper white in the first place

The natural shade of teeth is set by the dentin under the enamel, and it runs from off-white to light yellow. That is normal. Whitening lightens the shade several steps. It does not turn teeth into bathroom tiles, and anyone promising that is promising something the material cannot do.

What in-clinic whitening does

Professional whitening uses a peroxide gel applied after the gums are protected. The gel breaks down stains inside the tooth structure over the session. Most patients see their shade lift noticeably in one visit, and some sensitivity for a day or two afterwards that settles on its own. Results generally hold for a year or more, longer with sensible habits.

In Delhi, in-clinic whitening typically costs between about 6,000 and 15,000 rupees depending on the system used and how many sessions the shade needs.

What whitening cannot touch

Fillings, crowns and veneers do not lighten with the teeth. A patient with visible front crowns needs to know this before whitening, because the treated teeth will move away from the crown colour. The honest plan is whitening first, then replacing mismatched restorations once the new shade has settled.

About those home remedies

Charcoal powders and aggressive scrubbing wear enamel down. Enamel does not grow back, and thinner enamel eventually shows more yellow, the opposite of what you paid for. Baking soda used hard does the same. The strawberry recipes circulating online earn their results by removing surface stain and some surface enamel along with it.

Whitening toothpastes are the mildest of the lot. They polish surface stains from tea and coffee. That is genuinely useful, and genuinely limited. They will not reach the deeper staining that makes teeth look dull.

The boring things that protect a white smile

Rinse or drink water after tea and coffee. Skip the cigarettes, which stain faster than any gel removes. Get a scaling and polishing done every six to twelve months, which alone restores a surprising amount of brightness. Whitening is a boost on top of clean teeth. It is not a substitute for them.

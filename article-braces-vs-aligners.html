Braces or clear aligners: which one actually fits your teeth?

By Dr. Animesh Agarwal · Delhi Dental Cosmetics, GTB Nagar · 5 min read

Every week someone sits in the chair and asks one question: braces ya aligners? The honest answer is that both straighten teeth, and the right one depends on your case, your habits and your budget. Here is how we think it through with patients.

What braces do well

Fixed braces, metal or ceramic, are brackets and wires glued to the teeth. They move teeth continuously, day and night, whether you remember anything or not. For complicated movements, rotations, severe crowding, big vertical corrections, braces are still the most reliable tool. Ceramic versions make them far less visible than the metal smiles people remember from school.

What aligners do well

Clear aligners are removable plastic trays, changed every couple of weeks. They are nearly invisible, they come off for meals and brushing, and appointments are spaced further apart, which suits people who travel or work odd hours. The catch is on your side of the deal: aligners only work if you wear them twenty to twenty-two hours a day. Take them out too often, and the case drags on or the results fall short.

The questions that decide it

How complex is the case? Mild to moderate spacing or crowding usually suits aligners. Severe rotations, big bite corrections and cases needing tooth movement in several directions usually do better on braces.

How disciplined are you about wearing something removable? Be honest with yourself here. A patient who will keep taking aligners out for just this meal is better served by braces, which make that decision for them.

What does the budget look like? In Delhi, braces treatment typically starts around 30,000 to 40,000 rupees for metal and runs higher for ceramic. Aligners usually begin near that range and go up with case complexity. Both figures include the review visits that stretch across the treatment period.

What neither option fixes

Straightening teeth is only half the outcome. Retention is the other half. After either treatment, a retainer holds the teeth in their new positions. Skip the retainer and the teeth start drifting back, slowly and quietly. Patients sometimes come back two years later wondering why their treatment did not last. It lasted exactly as long as the retainer was worn.

How to decide in one visit

We take an X-ray and photographs, run through the options with real timelines and real numbers, and tell you which one we would choose for your mouth and why. The right answer is different for a college student, a bride with a fixed wedding date and a forty-year-old who wants nobody to notice anything.

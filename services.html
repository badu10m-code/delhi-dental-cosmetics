Treatments

Every treatment here starts the same way: an examination, an honest explanation of your options, and a price agreed before anything begins. Ask as many questions as you want.

Dental Implants

A titanium root placed where a tooth is missing, restored with a crown that looks and works like the real thing. Planned on the X-ray first, so you know the full cost and the number of visits before it starts.

A single implant with its crown typically lands between 25,000 and 55,000 rupees depending on the system and the bone. The full plan is written down before work begins. Read the implant guide →

Braces & Invisible Aligners

Metal, ceramic or clear aligners for crooked or crowded teeth. Treatment runs months, so the plan is written out visit by visit; you always know what is next and what it costs.

Metal braces usually start around 30,000 to 40,000 rupees; ceramic and clear aligners run higher, and the exact figure depends on your case. Read the braces vs aligners guide →

Smile Makeover & Veneers

The clinic's signature work. Zirconia crowns, veneers and full smile design, shaped to your face and shade-matched in person, never ordered from a shade card photo.

Root Canal Treatment

Infected or deep-decayed teeth saved where possible, single-sitting where the case allows it. Performed under proper anaesthesia; patients in reviews describe the RCTs as painless.

Most root canals in Delhi run 3,000 to 9,000 rupees for the treatment itself. The crown is quoted separately, and the total is told upfront. Read the root canal guide →

Security

Changing the passcode stores a new hash in the clinic cloud store. The old passcode keeps working as a fallback until the next website update.

Sessions end automatically after 20 minutes. After 5 wrong passcode attempts, this device waits 10 minutes. Appointment data keeps a local backup copy on this device in case the cloud store is briefly unreachable.

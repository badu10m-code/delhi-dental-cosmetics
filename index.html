The clinic

Built to keep nervous patients calm

A dental visit makes most people anxious. This clinic was arranged the other way around: soft lighting, calming music, a waiting area with sofas instead of plastic chairs, and a team that talks you through each step before it happens. Patients in reviews keep mentioning the same things: the ambience, the patience, and that the price was agreed before the work started.

Wheelchair accessible, with parking nearby and about seven seats in the waiting area, so appointments run on time.

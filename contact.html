Questions patients ask before coming

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Appointments are required before visiting, so the doctor you need has proper time for you. Request a slot through the form above or call during clinic hours.

Where exactly is the clinic?

2413 Basement, Hudson Lane, Kingsway Camp, GTB Nagar, Delhi 110009, about a minute from GTB Nagar Metro Station Gate No. 4.

What are the timings?

Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 1:30 PM and 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM. Sunday is closed.

Is the clinic wheelchair accessible?

Yes. The clinic is on the ground level of the building and wheelchair accessible.

Which payments do you accept?

Cash, and Paytm is accepted at the clinic. Carry a card as backup for larger treatment plans.

How much does a consultation cost?

Consultation is charged per visit and depends on the treatment planned. The fee and the full treatment cost are explained before any work begins.

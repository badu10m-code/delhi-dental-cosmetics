Your data

Privacy, in plain language

What this website collects, what it does not, and who can see it.

What the booking form collects

Only what the clinic needs to confirm your appointment: your name, phone number, an optional email address, your preferred date and time, the treatment you are asking about, and anything you choose to add in the note. Nothing else is asked and nothing else is stored.

Where it goes

Booking requests are sent to the clinic's own cloud store, which only the clinic staff can open through a passcode-protected dashboard. The clinic uses it to call or message you back and confirm your slot. It is not shared with advertisers, data brokers or anyone else.

What this website does not do

Public pages of this site set no tracking cookies, run no analytics, and show no ads. Nothing follows you to another website. When you tap a WhatsApp or call button, that opens your own WhatsApp or phone app, and their own policies apply from that point.

How long your details are kept

Booking requests stay in the clinic dashboard until the visit is done or you ask for them to be removed, whichever comes first. Ask the clinic by phone or WhatsApp and any record of your request will be deleted.
